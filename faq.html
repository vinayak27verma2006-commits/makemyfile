Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files uploaded?

The tools are designed to process supported files locally in your browser. Normal file processing does not require uploading your document to a MakeMyFile server.

Can I compress an image to a specific size?

Yes. The image compressor lets you enter a target size such as 20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB or 200 KB. The result depends on the original image and selected format.

Can I resize an image?

Yes. Enter the desired width and height and choose an output format.

Can I convert JPG, PNG and WebP?

Yes. The Image Converter supports JPG, PNG and WebP output.

Can I create a PDF from images?

Yes. JPG to PDF accepts multiple supported images and combines them into one PDF.

Can I merge PDFs?

Yes. Select two or more PDFs and use the Merge PDFs tool.

Can I extract PDF pages?

Yes. The Split PDF tool accepts ranges such as 1-3, 5 and 8-10.

Does PDF compression always make a file smaller?

No. Compression depends on the PDF's contents. Photo-heavy or scanned PDFs generally have more opportunity for size reduction than already-optimized PDFs.
